Coach states are simulated. No microphone or audio is used.

  1. Mock for context only. Amounts are invented. Only the Sponky card is wired.

  2. The coach is the main control. Idle is static, so it never looks like recording. Typing is always available.

  3. Tap to start, tap to stop and send. No press-and-hold, no always-on listening. Motion will follow real microphone level later; here it is simulated.

  4. Processing has its own non-audio motion and a text label. Use Next to continue.

  5. A direct request ('just show me') skips this step. 'Speak instead' stops playback, then starts listening.

  6. The activity opens under the coach; the coach stays. Nothing hints at the answer. Speech is not needed to play.

  7. This is already a complete visit. The guess controls collapsed into this compact result. A wrong guess gets a friendly reveal, not a failure animation.

  8. Brief check-in. Works by tap or by voice. Return-day data is a separate invented fixture.

  9. One brief celebration, then calm. No money animation: nothing was saved or moved.
